How We Scaled Our Database to 10M Users

When we hit 1M users, our Postgres instance started showing signs of strain. Here's what we did.

Read Replicas

We added three read replicas and shifted all analytics queries to them. This cut primary load by 60%.

Connection Pooling

PgBouncer in transaction mode brought connection overhead down from ~800ms to under 10ms.

Caching

Redis in front of the most-read tables reduced database hits by another 40%.

These three changes bought us another 18 months of headroom with minimal code changes.
