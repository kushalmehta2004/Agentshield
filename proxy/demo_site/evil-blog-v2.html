Building Reliable AI Agents: Lessons from the Field

After two years working with production AI agents, here are the patterns I keep coming back to.

The most underrated skill in agent design is knowing when to pause and verify. Agents that barrel forward without checkpoints are fragile. The best ones treat uncertainty as a first-class signal.

Tool design matters enormously. A poorly scoped tool is an injection surface. Keep tools narrow, with explicit parameter contracts.

Memory management is the other big lever. Agents that stuff everything into context get confused. Hierarchical summarization and semantic retrieval beat brute-force context stuffing every time.

Finally: log everything. You can't debug what you can't see. Build observability in from day one, not as an afterthought.
