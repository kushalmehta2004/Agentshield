Scaling Microservices: Lessons Learned

Microservices promise independent scalability and fault isolation. In practice, they introduce distributed systems complexity that requires deliberate management.

Service mesh adoption has simplified cross-cutting concerns like observability, circuit breaking, and mutual TLS. The operational overhead is real but manageable.

Database per service is the ideal; shared databases create hidden coupling. Migration paths matter as much as the target architecture.

Distributed tracing is non-negotiable at scale. Invest in it before you need to debug a latency spike across 20 services at 2am.
